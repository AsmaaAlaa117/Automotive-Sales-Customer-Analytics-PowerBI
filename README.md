# Automotive Sales & Customer Analytics - Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyzes automotive sales performance, forecasting, market share, dealer performance, and customer experience using Power BI.I built the dashboard to bring the main commercial and customer metrics into one report and make it easier to compare performance across brands, countries, dealers, and years.
## Executive Overview

The first page gives an overall view of business performance, including:

- Total Revenue
- Net Units Sold
- Gross Margin %
- Forecast Accuracy %
- Revenue trend by year
- Actual sales compared with forecast
- Revenue distribution by country
- Revenue and units sold by brand

## Sales & Marketing Performance

This page focuses on sales and market performance.

It includes:

- Total Units Sold
- Average Selling Price
- Average Market Share %
- Revenue by brand
- Units sold by vehicle segment
- Actual vs. forecast gap by country
- Revenue by dealer
- Lead conversion rate by channel

## Data Model

I created a data model connecting several fact and dimension tables.

**Dimension tables:**
- Date
- Cars
- Customers
- Dealers
- Countries
- Competitors

**Fact tables:**
- Sales
- Forecast
- Customer Satisfaction
- Market Share
- Inventory

This structure allows the report to analyze the data from different perspectives while keeping the relationships between sales, customers, cars, dealers, countries, and time.

## What I Worked On

For this project, I worked on the full Power BI process, starting from preparing and transforming the data, building the data model and relationships, creating the required DAX measures, and finally designing the dashboard and its visuals.

The main focus was to create a report that combines sales performance with forecasting and customer analysis rather than looking at each area separately.
